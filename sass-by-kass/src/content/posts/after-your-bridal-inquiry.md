Booking wedding hair shouldn't feel like a mystery. Here is exactly what happens after you send Kass a bridal inquiry.

## 1. You tell her about the day

The [bridal inquiry form](/bridal-inquiry) asks for the basics: your date (if you have one), where you're getting ready, how many people need hair and the styles you love. If something isn't decided yet, "not sure yet" is a perfectly good answer.

## 2. Kass replies by email

She reads every inquiry herself, checks her availability for your date and answers your questions. This is the moment to talk through which package fits you and your party.

## 3. Contract and deposit

Once you've chosen your package, Kass sends your contract and lets you know how to send your deposit. Your date is reserved once those are taken care of — sending the inquiry on its own doesn't book the day or take any payment.

## 4. Getting ready for the morning

From there, it's all about the details: inspiration photos, accessories and the plan for your morning.
