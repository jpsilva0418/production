You don't need every detail decided to send a bridal inquiry — but a few answers make planning much easier for you and for your stylist.

## Who needs hair?

The bride, of course — and then bridesmaids, mothers, grandmothers, flower girls? A rough headcount is enough to start the conversation.

## Where is everyone getting ready?

A hotel suite, a family home, the venue itself? Knowing the town or venue helps plan the morning.

## What's the look?

Updo, soft waves, half-up, or open to ideas? Save the photos you keep coming back to — they say more than any description.

## Are you wearing a veil or accessories?

Veils, combs, pins and flowers all affect how a style is built. Mention them early.

## Do you want a trial?

Many brides like to see their style before the day. If you're unsure, say so — it's a good question to talk through.
