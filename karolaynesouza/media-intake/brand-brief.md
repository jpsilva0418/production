## Observed brand style

Drawn from her Linktree theme, Square color tokens, the Canva price list, and the portrait / lash photo in this package (Instagram’s full grid was not downloadable; JP’s screenshot and highlight titles were used only as context):

- **Palette:** cream / ivory (`#fcf9ef` on Linktree text; flyer cream is approximately `#f5eee6`), black (`#000000` backgrounds and type), charcoal button outline (`#434343`), gold (`#d3ae1b` on the Square site; richer gold on the flyer). Linktree is a dark (black) page with cream type; the price list is a light luxury flyer with black and gold.
- **Type:** high-contrast serif monogram (KS) + elegant serif/script pairing on “Tabela de preços / Price List”; Linktree uses the Oxanium family; Square uses Square Market.
- **Vibe:** Brazilian lash & brow artist, “acabamento premium e simetria perfeita,” luxury/elegance rather than playful neon. Bilingual, WhatsApp-first booking, close-up eye work on skin (not heavy filters), dark or neutral studio backdrops.
- **Photography in hand:** a warm-neutral headshot of Karolayne (long dark hair, black top, gold necklace, nose stud) and a tight client lash/brow close-up. Highlight titles imply more content for lashes, brows, combo sets, the studio (“My space 🖤✨”), lash removal, and intro/personal highlights (“Hi, Girls”, “Ready”). JP’s description of the live grid: close-up client lash/brow photos on dark/neutral backgrounds plus studio space (ring light, treatment bed). Those grid photos are **not in this zip** because Instagram blocked unauthenticated media downloads.
- **Shapes:** Square site uses pill buttons (100px radius); Linktree uses large rounded outline buttons.
