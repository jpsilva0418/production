How to gather bridal hair inspiration that actually helps

A few simple ways to collect inspiration photos that make your wedding hair conversation easy.

Draft for Kass to review before launch.

Inspiration photos are the fastest way to show your stylist what you love. A little curation goes a long way.

Save more than one angle

A front view, a side view and a back view of a style you like tell a much fuller story than a single photo.

Notice what you keep choosing

Are your saves all soft and romantic? Sleek and polished? Mostly worn up, or mostly down? The pattern matters more than any one picture.

Include your dress and accessories

A photo of your neckline, veil or hair accessories helps the hairstyle and the outfit work together.

Share links, not just screenshots

Pinterest boards and Instagram posts can be added straight to the bridal inquiry form — one link per line.
