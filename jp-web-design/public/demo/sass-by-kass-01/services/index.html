Signature services
No. I

Hand-Tied Extensions

Hand-tied wefts sewn onto a fine bead row — flat, light and nearly invisible. The result is seamless length and density that blends with your natural hair and holds up to real life.

  • Move-up appointments as your hair grows
  • A simple care routine explained before you leave
