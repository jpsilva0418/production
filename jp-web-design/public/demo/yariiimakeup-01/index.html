About

Meet Yari.

Yaribel Vasquez · Professional makeup artist

Yaribel Vasquez, Yari to her clients, is the makeup artist and hairstylist behind YariiiMakeup, based in Poinciana, Florida. She does makeup, hairstyles, brows and lashes for weddings, Sweet 16s, proms and graduations, photoshoots and the pageant stage.

She also teaches, from a beginner’s makeup course to professional training for artists.
