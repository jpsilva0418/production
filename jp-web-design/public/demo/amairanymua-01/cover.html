meet your artist

Hi, she’s Amairany.

Amairany Sierra is a luxury hair and makeup artist based in Florida, working across Orlando and Tampa. She has been doing makeup since 2013 and trained at the Aveda Institute in Winter Park, graduating in 2018.

Her signature is what she calls real-life-filter skin: complexion work that looks like skin in person and in photographs, never cakey. From there the look goes wherever you want it — soft glam, full glam, glam waves.

Wedding mornings are her focus: close attention to detail, and a calm room while you get ready.

asked & answered

The quick answers.

How do I book my wedding?

Start with the bridal inquiry form. It asks for your date, ceremony time, getting-ready location and how many people need hair and makeup, so the reply you get is about your day. Already know what you want? Bridal services can be booked directly.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. Trials are part of the bridal services. Mention yours on the inquiry form and it gets planned around your date.

Can you do hair and makeup for my bridal party?

Yes. The inquiry form asks how many people are getting ready with you, so the morning can be timed properly.

Do you come to me?

Appointments are offered both in studio and on location across Orlando and Tampa. For anything further afield, ask on the inquiry form.

I'm not a bride. Can I still book?

Of course. Event and everyday glam has its own booking page, with in-studio and travel options.

What does “real-life filter” mean?

It is how Amairany describes her skin work: smooth, even and luminous, but still skin. Soft glam or full glam, it should never look cakey.

Where can I see pricing?

Current packages and pricing live on her booking page, so they are always up to date.

Secure your wedding day glam.

Tell Amairany your date, your vision and who is getting ready with you.
