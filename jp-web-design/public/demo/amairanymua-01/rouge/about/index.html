The story
Behind the brushes

Amairany Sierra is a luxury hair and makeup artist based in Florida, working across Orlando and Tampa. She has been doing makeup since 2013 and trained at the Aveda Institute in Winter Park, graduating in 2018.

Her signature is what she calls real-life-filter skin: complexion work that still looks like skin, in person and in photographs.

“Real-life filter skin. Never cakey.”

From there the look goes wherever you want it: soft glam, full glam, glam waves. She works with brides and the people getting ready with them, and with clients dressing for events and photoshoots.

Based in Orlando + Tampa, Florida. Appointments in studio or on location.
