Good to know

Before you inquire.

How do I book my wedding?

Start with the bridal inquiry. It asks for your date, ceremony time, getting-ready location and how many people need hair and makeup, so the reply you get is about your day. Already know what you want? Bridal services can be booked directly.

Do you offer bridal trials?

Yes. Trials are part of the bridal services. Mention yours in the inquiry and it is planned around your date.

Can you do hair and makeup for my bridal party?

Yes. The inquiry asks how many people are getting ready with you, so the morning can be timed properly.

Do you come to me?

Appointments are offered both in studio and on location across Orlando + Tampa. For anything further afield, ask in the inquiry.

What does “real-life filter” mean?

It is how Amairany describes her skin work: skin that still looks like skin, in person and on camera. Soft glam or full glam, never cakey.

Where can I see pricing?

Current packages and pricing live on her booking page, so they are always up to date.

I’m not a bride. Can I still book?

Of course. Event and everyday glam has its own booking page, with in-studio and travel options.
