How to prepare for your glam

A clean face, hair washed and blown out the day before, and what her packages already include.

Start your bridal inquiry →Book event glam

Getting ready with Amairany starts before the brushes come out. Here is what she asks of you before your appointment, the preview her packages already include, her word on extensions, and what stays with you after the ceremony.

Before your appointment

She asks for two things, in her words:

  1. 01“Please come in with a clean face.”
  2. 02“Hair should be washed and blown out a day before your appointment.”

Your preview comes first

Preview sessions are offered for hair and for makeup, and her packages already include yours. If you would rather see the glam before you sign, a trial can be booked first.

“Preview sessions are available for both hair and makeup. Our packages already include your preview, but you are always welcome to book a trial session prior to signing if you prefer.”

Amairany Sierra

Extensions, for glam waves

Planning glam waves? Ask her about extensions. She rents them, or points you to the ones worth buying.

“For max volume & longevity with glam waves, hair extensions are highly recommended. You can either rent them directly with me or I can guide you toward the best extensions to purchase.”

Amairany Sierra

After the ceremony

Touch-ups are part of her packages. As listed on her booking page:

  • The Luxe BrideBride only · hair & makeupDeluxe touch-up kit · 30 min post-ceremony touch-ups
  • The Signature SoireeBride + 31 hour post-ceremony touch-ups · Luxury touch-up kits
  • The Luxe GalaBride + 51 hour post-ceremony touch-ups
  • The Radiant BrideBride only · makeup only1 hour touch-up · Luxury touch-up kit
  • The Luxe GlamBride + 4 · makeup only1 hour touch-up
  • Glam HairBride + 4 · hair onlyHair touch-ups
