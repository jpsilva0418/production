Amairany keeps her list short. Before your appointment, she asks for two things.

  1. 01

    “Please come in with a clean face.”

  2. 02

    “Hair should be washed and blown out a day before your appointment.”

The look, before the day

Every one of her bridal packages includes a preview of the look before the wedding day: the hair-and-makeup collections open with two-part bridal artistry, a private preview session and then the wedding-day application. And if you would rather try first, a trial can be booked before you sign.

Preview sessions are available for both hair and makeup. Our packages already include your preview, but you are always welcome to book a trial session prior to signing if you prefer.

Amairany

Glam waves that last

For the most volume and longevity in glam waves, Amairany highly recommends hair extensions. You can rent them directly from her, or she can guide you toward the best extensions to buy. Hair extension rental is included in The Luxe Gala, and Glam Hair includes an extension install for the bride.

For max volume & longevity with glam waves, hair extensions are highly recommended.

Amairany
