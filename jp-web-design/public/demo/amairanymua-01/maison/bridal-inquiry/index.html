    Good to know

    Before you inquire.

    In her words: travel, dates, extensions, trials and photography.

    Do you travel?

    Yes, I travel all across Central Florida & out of state.

    Can you hold my date?

    Unfortunately no. Dates cannot be held without a signed contract & a non-refundable retainer.

    Do you recommend hair extensions?

    For max volume & longevity with glam waves, hair extensions are highly recommended. You can either rent them directly with me or I can guide you toward the best extensions to purchase.

    Is there a booking minimum?

    I do not have a booking minimum for hair & makeup.

    Do you offer trials?

    Yes! Preview sessions are available for both hair and makeup. Our packages already include your preview, but you are always welcome to book a trial session prior to signing if you prefer.

    Do you offer photography?

    Photography is a separate service: my fiancé is a photographer. He offers film / polaroid photography for weddings & special events.
