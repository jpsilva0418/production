Amairany Sierra, three directions.

One site, built on the same system as Sass by Kass: seven pages, the same words, her photographs and her own booking links. A direction is a colour palette and an opening. Open each one on your phone, watch the opening, then pick the one to finish.

The same in all three

  • Seven pages Home, About, Bridal, Services, Portfolio, Reviews and the Bridal Inquiry.
  • The opening Her name is drawn inside a ring, travels to its place, and the page assembles around it in under three seconds. Once per visit; any tap or scroll ends it.
  • Stills and film Her photographs move through the hero and the galleries with film cutting in. 4 film slots are marked and waiting for her reels: no stand-in video anywhere.
  • Bridal inquiry On the site, in four short steps, asking her own form’s questions. Demo mode: nothing is sent, and the last step hands off to her real form.
  • Her booking Event bookings and bridal services open her own booking pages, at the addresses on her link page.

The small marker at the foot of every page switches between A, B and C on the same page, so any two can be compared screen for screen.
