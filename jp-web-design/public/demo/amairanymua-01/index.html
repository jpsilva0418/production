In all three

  • Seven pages Home, About, Bridal, Services, Portfolio, Reviews and the Bridal Inquiry.
  • Book in one tap “Bridal inquiry” and “Book event glam” are on the first screen of every page and stay within reach as you scroll.
  • Bridal inquiry On the site, in four short steps, asking her own form’s questions. Demo mode: nothing is sent, and the last step hands off to her real form.
  • Her booking Event bookings and bridal services open her own booking pages, at the addresses on her link page.
  • Stills and film Her photographs move through the hero and the portfolio with film cutting in. 4 film spots are marked and waiting for her reels: no stand-in video anywhere.

The small marker at the foot of every page switches between 1, 2 and 3 on the same page, so any two can be compared screen for screen. The opening plays once per visit: add ?intro=1 to replay it.
