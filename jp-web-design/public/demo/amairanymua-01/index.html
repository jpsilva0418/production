In every one

  • Book in one tap “Bridal inquiry” and “Book event glam” are on the first screen of every page and stay within reach as you scroll.
  • Bridal inquiry On the site, in short steps, asking her own form’s questions with her own answer choices. Demo mode: nothing is sent, and the last step hands off to her real form.
  • Her booking Event bookings and bridal services open her own booking pages, at the addresses on her link page.
  • Her links Instagram, TikTok and her Google reviews open in one tap, and her WeddingWire and The Knot ratings link to the reviews themselves.
  • Stills and film Her photographs move through the hero and the portfolio with her own clips playing among them (from her TikTok, silent). Where her caption credits another artist, the clip and the photo say so.
  • Pages Rounds two and three: Home, Portfolio, Bridal, About, a Journal with two sample articles, and the Bridal Inquiry (Rouge keeps a tighter five). Round one: Home, About, Bridal, Services, Portfolio, Reviews and the Bridal Inquiry.

The small marker at the foot of every page switches between 1 and 9 on the same page where that page exists, so any two can be compared screen for screen. The opening plays once per visit: add ?intro=1 to replay it.
