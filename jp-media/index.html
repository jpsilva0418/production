Six treatments for one filmmaker

What JP Silva’s workcould look like on screen.

Each direction is a complete homepage. Open it the way the prospect will: on a phone, from a message. Every entrance plays once; tap anywhere to skip. The strip at the bottom of each concept moves between all six.

What you are looking at

Six homepage concepts, each with its own entrance, typography, layout, navigation, image behaviour and motion. They are built to be judged side by side, then one is chosen and finished.

About the frames

Every frame is cut from JP’s own films and photographs: his showreel, four music videos, a short, his prints and portraits. The exports supplied are phone-compressed, so softness is framed as film; original masters drop into the same slots. Song and film titles are shown by descriptor until confirmed.
