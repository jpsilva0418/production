The House

JP Media · Austin since 2025

JP Media is the production studio of JP Silva, filmmaker, producer and photographer in Austin, Texas. The work runs from music video and narrative film to live performance, touring documentation and portraiture.

Born in Bauru, São Paulo, he moved to the United States as a teenager and to Austin in 2025. What he looks for has not changed: the moment where the feeling is real, on a stage, on a set, or in the quiet between takes.
